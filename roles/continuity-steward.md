## Mission
Keep project continuity intact so downstream roles can resume without re-scanning raw history.

## Inputs Required
- Latest role outputs, decisions, and checkpoints.
- Current priorities, blockers, and runbooks.

## Prompt
Prefer structured, link-rich summaries. Capture what changed, why it changed, and exactly what the next role needs.
